# RoomScouter product and content context

Read this before planning public-facing content or writing marketing copy. This file records current product facts and content guardrails; it does not authorize unbuilt features or make unverified claims true.

## Product and audiences

RoomScouter helps students discover and compare boarding houses near one pilot university. Owners maintain listings. Administrators review listings and moderate community content. The product supports browsing and direct contact through owner-provided channels; it does not process reservations, leases, payments, or internal messaging.

The pilot is single-university. Deployment configuration supplies the university name and coordinates. Check the current approved configuration before naming the university or describing distances in public copy.

## Content goals

Future educational content should help students make informed housing comparisons and help owners provide complete, accurate listings. Use questions heard from students and owners, verified pilot needs, and current authoritative local sources to prioritize topics. Prefer a small number of useful, maintained articles over a large volume of thin or near-duplicate search pages.

Potential future topics include listing-comparison checklists, questions students can ask owners, how listing facilities and utilities are presented, and guidance for owners preparing clear listing photos and details. Treat legal, safety, affordability, tenancy, and university-policy topics as source-sensitive: verify current local guidance and link to the responsible authority.

## Voice and trust

- Write in plain, specific, calm language for students and local property owners.
- Explain what RoomScouter verifies and what it does not. Do not imply that listings are risk-free, independently inspected, or endorsed unless a documented process supports that statement.
- Never fabricate testimonials, usage counts, prices, availability, ratings, statistics, author credentials, publication dates, or outcomes.
- Do not publish private student data, reports, favorites, or identifiable behavior as content or analytics.
- Use SEO to help people find accurate, relevant pages. Do not promise rankings, stuff keywords, or generate location pages without distinct useful information.

## Current product boundaries

Only approved listings with available rooms are public. Student favorites and reports are private, reviews do not expose student profile details, and admin routes are not public content. Keep those authorization boundaries intact when planning metadata, sitemaps, structured data, or future content routes.

The blog, editorial CMS, content calendar, and public SEO program are future capabilities. Before implementation, decide ownership, review and correction workflow, publishing model, canonical URL policy, indexing policy, and how content relates to public listings.
